Personal Tools

The Google Cloud app shown on the sign-in screen as “Investment Advisor App”.

This is a private, single-user app. Its owner uses it on their own computer to work with their own Google accounts: uploading and scheduling videos on YouTube channels they own, and reading their own channel analytics.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not sell, share or advertise anything.
