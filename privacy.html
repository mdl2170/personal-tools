Privacy Policy

For the Google Cloud app “Investment Advisor App” (Personal Tools). Last updated October 28, 2026.

Who uses this app

Only its owner, on their own computer, signed in to Google accounts they own. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted on Google's sign-in screen, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, captions and publishing schedule on the owner's own channels, and to read those channels' own statistics.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer. The app keeps no other copy, runs no server, and collects no data about anyone else.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data from Google APIs is used only for the features described above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored token stop working.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
